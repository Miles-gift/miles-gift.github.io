# 雅思写作笔记

## 第一部分 基本注意事项

**考场注意事项：**

1. 考试总时长为==1h==，时间分布大致为20min+40min;
2. 打分采用==退分制==，分数分布为$\frac{1}{3}task1+\frac{2}{3}task2$，计算结果为作文总分
3. 想获得高分得注意**GRA**，即准确性、3类从句和分词的使用

**LR：**

准确；less-common words

#### 2.4 BODY段写作

##### 2.4.1 趋势描述

方法一：verb. + adv.

> The number of smoking males **decreased** <u>gradually</u> from 750 thousand to 210 thousand between 1965 and 2010.
>
> The number of smoking males **decreased** ==**to**== 210 thousand between 1965 and 2010. 【到】
>
> The number of smoking males **decreased** ==**by**== 540 thousand between 1965 and 2010. 【‘了’、写差值】

方法二：there be

> There was a gradual **decrease** ==in== the number of smoking males from 750 thousand to 210 thousand between 1965 and 2010.
>
> There was a gradual **decrease** ==**to**== 210 thousand ==in== the number of smoking males between 1965 and 2010.【到】
>
> There was a gradual **decrease** ==**of**== 540 thousand ==in== the number of smoking males between 1965 and 2010.【‘了’、写差值】

### 三、静态图写作方法

静态图可能会出现：bar chart、pie chart、table

#### 3.1 文章框架

1. 开头段：参考title进行改写

3. 结尾段 (overview)

#### 3.2 BODY写作方法

##### 3.2.1 基本要点

**step1：**关注最值：最大值、最小值、第二大值；

**step2：**按照数据的相似性进行分组，要求尽量涵盖全部数据；

##### 3.2.2 分数表示

1/4 = one/a quarter

3/4 = three quarters

1/3 = one third = one in three

2/3 = two thirds

##### 3.2.3 句子合并

###### （1）and

> The percentage of water used in agricultural sector <u>in South East Asia, Africa and Central Asia</u> was much higher than other areas, **and** over 80% of water was allocated to watering farmland/irrigation.

###### （2）where 定语从句

> Compared with other three areas, a higher percentage of water was used in agricultural sector in South East Asia, Africa and Central Asia, **where** over 80% of water was allocated to watering farmland/irrigation.
>
> Compared with other two items, the percentage of spending on food, drink and tobacco was the highest, especially in **Turkey** where its consumers allocated just under one thrid of total expenditure on these consumer goods

###### （3）v-ing

> South America was also an agricultural area, distributing just under three quarters of water in this sector.

##### 5.2.2 语法与润色

###### （1）语法注意事项

遇到建筑noun【C】单数时：

- 首次提及、新建筑 + a/an、one
- 再次提及 + the

###### （2）表达润色

1）润色1

A fountain was in the center/middle of this park.

【倒装】In the center/middle of this park was a fountain.

【倒装+which】In the center/middle of this park was a fountain, **which** was to the right of a stage for musicians.

2）润色2

There were rose gardens around the edges of the park. $\Rightarrow$  there be 句型中介词短语提前：

Around the edges of the park there were rose gardens. $\Rightarrow$ there be 句型中介词短语提前 + which：

Around the edges of the park there were rose gardens, which were close to seats. ✅
