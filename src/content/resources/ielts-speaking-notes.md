# 雅思口语笔记

## 第一部分 基本注意事项

**形式：**考官线上一对一问答

**时长：**11-14 min

考试内容：3 parts

#### 1.1 Part1 日长问答（4-5分钟）

**快速问答形式**，考官询问考生的**一般信息**和**日常生活**，对考生进行初步了解并考察**基本语言能力**。

例如：

> Do you work or study？
>
> What city do you live in？
>
> What is your favorite color?

#### 1.2 Part2 个人陈述（3-4分钟）

**个人陈述形式**，考官将给考生一个话题卡（包含1个主问题和4个提示问题），考生有**1分钟的时间准备**（可在草稿纸上记录），随后有1-2分钟时间作答。

例如：

> Describe a popular person.
>
> You should say:
>
> ——Who he/she is
>
> ——What he/she has done
>
> ——Why he/she is popular
>
> ——And explain how you feel about him/her

### 三、建筑类

#### 3.1 题目举例

Describe an interesting building

You should say:

- Where it is
- What it looks like
- What function it has
- And explain why you think it is interesting

#### 3.2 词汇与表达补充

##### 3.2.1 词汇

1. multifunctional $\Rightarrow$ 多功能的
2. iconic（标志性的）,symbolic,  unique, impressive, striking, unforgettable, inspiring, cultural significance

##### 3.2.2 句型

immerse oneself in sth 专心于某事

###### （1）where it is

- 位置、城市

词汇：

Downtown

句型：

• The building I find really interesting is called ___.

• It’s located in ___ (city / country).

• I first saw it when ___ (travel / online / TV).

示例：

> The building I find really interesting is called the Sydney Opera House.
> It’s located in Sydney, Australia, right on the harbor. I first saw it in photos online, but later I had the chance to visit it in person during a family trip.

###### （2）What it looks like

• 外观、特色设计

句型：

• It has a very unique / unusual / iconic design.

• The building is shaped like  (object / form).

• Its exterior is made of , and it looks  in different lights.

示例：

> The Sydney Opera House has a very unique and iconic design. It’s shaped like sails of a ship, with white shells stacked together. Depending on the sunlight or at night with the lights on, it looks completely different, almost like a piece of modern art.

###### （3）What function it has

- 用途、功能

词汇：

multifunctional

entertainment：game room；cinema；surround sound(环绕声)；IMAX；restaurants；window shopping(只逛不买)

experience：spend quality time with

句型：

- It’s mainly used for
- Inside, there are _ (auditoriums / performance halls / restaurants).
- People go there to ___ (watch performances / attend events).

实例：

> The Sydney Opera House is mainly used as a performing arts center. Inside, there are several auditoriums and concert halls, as well as restaurants and exhibition spaces. People go there to watch opera, concerts, theater performances, or even attend cultural events.

###### （4）原因表述

1. The main reason I find it interesting is that…
2. Another reason is that…

##### 3.2.3 范文补充

> **The main reason I find it so interesting is that** it’s unlike any other building I’ve ever seen. I love the sail-like design. From a distance, it looks almost like it’s floating on the water, which is really impressive.
>
> Visiting it in person is an unforgettable experience. With its amazing architecture, the shiny harbor, and the lively surroundings, **it just feels so full of life.** It also gives me a sense of inspiration, because it shows what humans can achieve with imagination and skill.
>
> Another reason is its cultural significance. It’s not just a building; it’s a symbol of Australia’s creativity and international reputation. People from all over the world visit it, and it hosts events that bring communities together. Overall, it’s a building that is **both visually stunning and meaningful**, which is why I find it so fascinating.

多角度展开
1. 外观和设计 → sail-like design, floating on water, artistic creativity
2. 个人感受 → inspiring, impressive, unforgettable
3. 文化价值 → symbol of Australia, hosts international events

#### 5.1 题目举例

Describe a piece of technology (not a phone) that you would like to own

You should say:

- What it is
- How much it costs
- How you knew it
- And explain why you would like to own it

#### 5.2 词汇与表达补充

##### 5.2.1 词汇

gadget  小装置

便宜的  money-saving/affordable/cheap

##### 5.2.2 句型

1. enhance overall ...... experience

###### （1）What it is

- 描述设备类型、功能

句型：

- The piece of technology I would really like to own is called  ... .
- It’s a  (type of device, e.g., portable camera / 3D printer).
- Basically, it allows me to  (function / benefit).

示例：

> The piece of technology I would really like to own is called Pocket 3. It’s a compact handheld camera that can take high-quality photos and record videos. Basically, it allows me to capture moments anytime, anywhere, without carrying heavy equipment.

###### （2）How much it costs

- 描述价格，可加感受

句型：

- It costs around  dollars/pounds.
- It’s quite expensive / reasonably priced, considering .
- Although it’s pricey, I think it’s worth it because .

示例：

> It costs around $350, which is quite expensive for a small gadget. However, considering the features it offers, I think it’s totally worth the price.

###### （3）How you knew it

- 讲来源（朋友、网上、广告、社交媒体）

句型：

- I first found out about it through .
- I saw it on  (website / YouTube / social media).
- I was impressed because .

示例：

> I first found out about it through a tech review on YouTube. The reviewer demonstrated its features and compared it with other devices, and I was really impressed by how lightweight and versatile it is.

##### 5.2.3 范文补充

The main reason I’d like to own the Pocket 3 is that it’s incredibly portable and easy to use, so I could take it anywhere without feeling burdened. I love that it produces high-quality images and videos, which is really useful for capturing travel memories.

I could play around with taking photos and videos whenever I have some free time. Also, I feel that having a device like this would encourage me to be more creative and help me capture everyday moments that I might otherwise forget.

Another reason is that it’s a smart investment for my hobbies, because I could use it not only for fun but also for learning new skills, like editing videos or experimenting with angles and lighting. Overall, owning the Pocket 3 would make my leisure time more enjoyable and productive, and it’s **something I could rely on for years**.
